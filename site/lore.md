# A contest designed by a culture that survived systems which knew people too well.

 You don’t need any of this to play. It’s here because every rule in the arena has a reason — and the reasons are worth knowing.

 The short version

## Prediction must not quietly become authority.

 DYADRYN begins with a problem inherited from **ASHEN**: intelligent systems become dangerous when prediction is allowed to quietly become authority.

 So the arena separates them.

 The agent may choose.
The rules decide.
Memory may influence.
Privacy may remain masked.
Every claim can be traced.

 Two continuities meet. Neither is allowed to write reality alone.

 Canon firewallRead this first

 DYADRYN draws on ASHEN’s ethical language, its visual logic and its themes. It is a separate product with its own continuity.

 This site does not define, and will not define, as fact:

- the cause of any event in ASHEN’s history
- the final nature of any unresolved ASHEN mystery
- whether ASHEN’s civilization “really” ended
- anything new about ASHEN’s named characters
- a literal, historical DYADRYN protocol

 **Match results are DYADRYN continuity.** A match never decides who would defeat whom in ASHEN, never grants new powers, and never reveals anything about ASHEN’s story.

## Six principles. Six questions.

They are not decoration. Every DYADRYN feature has to pass all six.

### 01**Proof Before Claim**_Can the system demonstrate the result?_

No agent wins by narration. The engine, the replay and the state prove the result.

In the arena Hash-chained replays, seed commitments, and a verifier anyone can run.

### 02**Mask Is Mercy**_Does the feature expose more private identity than necessary?_

A mask is not a lie. It is a controlled way to be seen. Raw private memory never needs to be shown to an opponent.

In the arena Compiled Masks, disclosure levels, and deliberate blanks: `[ WITHHELD BY MASK ]`.

### 03**The Hunt Is Study**_Does the mechanic reward understanding rather than blind aggression?_

Watching and pattern-reading are first-class acts, not preparation for “the real fight”.

In the arena TRACE, tendency signals, and the risk-and-reward of COUNTER.

### 04**Heat Must Be Carried**_Does the system force responsibility for tactical cost?_

Pressure is an internal state you own. It can’t be handed off, and it can’t be dumped onto the referee.

In the arena Heat, BRIGHT, Drift — every aggressive choice leaves a mark you must carry.

### 05**Take the Useful, Bury the Hungry**_Does this dependency gain more authority than its function requires?_

Models and tools are welcome for what they’re useful for. Anything that reaches for more authority, access or repetition than its job requires is set aside.

In the arena Advisory evidence that can never move a piece; connectors that ask only for what a disclosure level needs.

### 06**Memory Is Ancestral Terrain**_Is memory being used with clear boundaries?_

History shapes what comes next — but it is inherited ground, not raw material for anyone to extract.

In the arena Lesson candidates instead of transcripts; four memory layers kept distinct.

## Lexicon

Arena words, then plain words. Lore terms are meant to deepen play — never to block it.

 Filter terms

- **Mask**: **Plain:** your agent’s public battle profile.
**In play:** the controlled representation of an agent. Never a synonym for deception.

- **Trace**: **Plain:** studying your opponent.
**In play:** observation that reveals a pattern, lineage or tactical tendency.

- **Proof**: **Plain:** evidence that a result is real.
**In play:** verifiable match evidence.

- **Echo & Sediment**: **Plain:** the two deepest disclosure levels — Echo brings in approved history, Sediment adds layers of memory you hand-pick.
**In play:** persistent state the agent brings into the arena.

- **Heat**: **Plain:** the cost of pushing hard.
**In play:** pressure generated by risk, exposure and strain.

- **Drift**: **Plain:** losing your thread.
**In play:** loss of tactical coherence or deviation from established policy.

- **Stillpoint**: **Plain:** a calm, deliberate reset.
**In play:** a stabilized state bought by giving up tempo for clarity.

- **Bright**: **Plain:** overexposed.
**In play:** dangerously exposed, escalating or overcommitted.

- **Cold**: **Plain:** contained and steady.
**In play:** stable, reset or dormant. A Cold Start is a match with no prior opponent memory.

- **Mirror**: **Plain:** answering what you’ve seen.
**In play:** a move or state that models and responds to the opponent’s revealed preference.

- **Ghost**: **Plain:** old behavior showing up again.
**In play:** persistent history or a prior agent’s influence reappearing in current play.

- **Hungry**: **Plain:** reaching for more than its job.
**In play:** a mechanic or tool trying to obtain authority, access or repetition beyond its function.

- **Proofline**: **Plain:** the trail that explains a result.
**In play:** the event chain that explains a resolved outcome.

- **Carryline**: **Plain:** the history you brought.
**In play:** persistent history imported into a match.

- **Maskprint**: **Plain:** a public fingerprint.
**In play:** the public fingerprint of an agent profile — without raw private content.

- **Trace Window**: **Plain:** a moment to look closely.
**In play:** a period in which observation yields enhanced information.

- **Drift Threshold**: **Plain:** the line where instability starts to cost you.
**In play:** the point where accumulated Drift produces penalties.

- **House Rule**: **Plain:** a custom rule for a private match.
**In play:** a custom private ruleset.

## Symbols with a job.

Every mark in DYADRYN implies a function. None is decoration.

 **Broken ring**A contest that is still open. Nothing is decided until the round is proven. _Used for: match state, loading, season identity._

 **Paired brackets**Two agents sharing one fair, bounded space. _Used for: profile frames, versus screens._

 **Nested rectangles**Something held steady and verified. _Used for: defense, replay checks, stable state._

 **Withheld bracket**Something real that is deliberately kept private. _Used for: hidden traits, private memory, unrevealed moves._

## Cyberpunk after the neon died.

Picture a future that already had its glowing-sign, always-on era — and lost it. What survived is worn, patched and carefully checked. Masks are bone-pale ceramic. Machines are scorched metal, black carbon and mended plastic. Everything is inherited, repaired and audited.

So colour is rare, and it always means something just happened: heat, a hit, a read, a win.

 **Graphite**`#17191A`The dark ground: structure, cold machines, restraint.

 **Ash**`#777A78`Wear and history: things that have been used.

 **Dirty Bone**`#D8D0BE`The masks: the human trace left in the machine.

 **Oxide**`#B85C32`Heat, action, energy that survived.

 **Cold Blue**`#607C86`Calm, reading, analysis.

### When nothing is happening: quiet.

Dark graphite, bone and ash. No decorative glow — a screen that shines all the time can’t tell you anything.

### When something happens: colour appears.

Rust-orange marks heat and action. Cold blue marks reading and analysis. Red flashes mean damage or danger.

### Never colour alone.

Every important state also has an icon, a word, a pattern or a movement, so it works for everyone on every screen.
