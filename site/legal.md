## Privacy

 This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and makes no third-party requests. Two comfort settings (larger text, reduced motion) are remembered in your browser’s local storage and never leave your device. The Training Ground and replay tools run entirely in your browser; nothing you do in them is sent anywhere.

## What DYADRYN does not publish

 Core intellectual property is protected by design. The public site explains every rule that changes a player’s decision, but does **not** publish the ranked engine’s constants or formulas, the Mask compiler’s allocation method, prompts, evidence question sets, schemas, credentials, or routing configuration. The Training Ground uses a simplified teaching model that is _not_ the ranked resolver, and every sample on this site is labelled as such.
